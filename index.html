Welcome to Where to Fly Next! A website designed for travelers, world explorers!
In this website you will be able to keep track of your voyages, the countries you have visited, as well as your travel times and costs! You will also be able to share your favorite destination, picture, and your most memorable experience! All that just for your eyes only!

Here are some helpful Travel Links

  • Lonely Planet will help you plan your next trip, and show you the attractions to not miss at every location! Lonely Planet will also provide you with book recounting other travelers stories and experiences.

  • Google Flights is a service from Google that will help you find flights for anywhere in the world.

  • Trip Advisor is one of the most visited and used websites for all travelers. This website provides the best deals and choices from flights, to hotels, restaurants and vacation rentals!
